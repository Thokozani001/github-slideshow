Method

  • Coverage: every high site carries a 5 km radius ring; a connected site is reported as inside or outside that ring using its great-circle distance from the high site it serves.
  • Connectivity: each connected site is joined to its high site with a link line, in the KMZ and on the map above.
  • Distances were recalculated from the coordinates and compared with the sheet. Nine sites come out more than 1 km different from the figure in the sheet; the differences are listed on the Data Quality sheet.
  • Two high sites had no coordinates in the sheet (Brits Library, Lethabong Maboloka Library). They are positioned by trilateration from their connected sites and the sheet's own distances, and flagged “estimated” in every file.
  • Three Delareyville sites have a distance but no high site anywhere in the sheet, so they are left unassigned rather than guessed.
  • Nothing in the source data was deleted: the original rows 40–145 are kept verbatim on the last sheet.
